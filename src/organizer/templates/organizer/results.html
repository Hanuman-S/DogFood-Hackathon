the final score combines the judged result and the community vote, each as a percentile rank within the event: final = judges% × judged percentile + community% × vote percentile. 100 / 0 is judges only. the weights are whole numbers adding up to 100, and they lock once judging or voting opens, so nobody judges or votes under weights that could still change. a final result with a community weight needs the vote to have closed; computing it freezes the tally it uses.
