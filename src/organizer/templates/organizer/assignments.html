how assignment works

assign fills every project up to the reviews-per-project target, and only tops up what is missing, so pressing it again is safe. a judge only gets projects in tracks they cover, never one they declined, never one you withdrew from them, and loads are balanced; among equal judges the choice is random, with the seed saved so a round can be reproduced exactly.

reshuffle unstarted draws every review nobody has started again, at random, with a new seed: use it to redo the assignment. started reviews (a draft or submitted) always stay with their judge.

to move one review: withdraw it from that judge, then press assign (it goes to someone else), or add a judge by hand under "manage".
