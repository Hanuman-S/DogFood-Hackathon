how assignment works

assign fills every project up to the reviews-per-project target, and only tops up what is missing, so pressing it again is safe. a judge only gets projects in tracks they cover, never one they declined, and loads are balanced; among equal judges the choice is random, with the seed saved so a round can be reproduced exactly.
