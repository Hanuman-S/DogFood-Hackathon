judges score every criterion from 1 to 5; each weight sets that criterion's share of the score.

how weights and levels work

weights are relative: each is a number above 0, and a criterion's share is its weight over the sum (1 / 1 / 1 is three exact thirds; 2 / 1 / 1 is a half and two quarters). the results use the weighted average. "levels" on a criterion describe what any of 1–5 means (optional; leave the rest empty).
